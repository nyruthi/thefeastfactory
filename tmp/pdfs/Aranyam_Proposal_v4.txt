01 / EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Executive Summary
The food and catering industry in India is undergoing a structural shift. Consumers increasingly expect to
discover, configure, and order catering services entirely through digital channels — on their own
schedule, with full transparency over pricing, menu options, and logistics. For a brand like Aranyam that
has built a strong reputation across multiple locations in Telangana, a dedicated digital platform is not an
optional enhancement. It is the next essential stage of business growth.
This proposal outlines the design and development of the Aranyam Smart Catering & Food Experience
Platform — a purpose-built Android and iOS mobile application and web-based admin dashboard that
enables Aranyam to serve customers directly, manage operations centrally, and build lasting customer
relationships without dependency on third-party aggregators.
The platform is not a generic food ordering application. It is designed specifically for the complexity of
large-format catering — guiding customers through event date, guest count, dietary preferences,
occasion type, and budget before intelligently recommending platters, menu combinations, and service
types that match their requirements. This is the experience that Aranyam's customers deserve, and that
no third-party platform can deliver for your specific business.
What This Platform Delivers
• A direct digital channel for catering and food orders — owned entirely by Aranyam.
• A guided, intelligent ordering experience that reduces enquiry calls and manual
coordination.
• Real-time operational visibility for the Aranyam team through a centralised admin
dashboard.
• The foundation for customer retention, repeat business, and data-driven growth.
• Complete independence from third-party commission structures.
We have already built the Aranyam website. We know the brand, the menu, the locations, and the
experience you want your customers to have. This platform is the natural and most significant next step
in Aranyam's digital journey — and we are the team best positioned to build it.

02 / UNDERSTANDING ARANYAM'S BUSINESS
Understanding Aranyam's Business
Aranyam is not a conventional quick-service restaurant. It is a destination dining and catering brand, built
on the identity of a jungle theme, authentic flavours, and the ability to serve large groups for significant
occasions — corporate events, family celebrations, weddings, and community gatherings. Amogham
Foods Pvt. Ltd. has successfully established Aranyam across multiple locations in Telangana, building a
customer base that values both the experience and the food.
Business Model
• Large-format catering for events, occasions, and group orders is a core revenue stream.
• Walk-in and dine-in service across multiple locations.
• A structured menu covering a wide range of categories — veg and non-veg — at defined
per-person pricing tiers.
• A growing customer base who return for occasions and recommend Aranyam to their networks.
Current Digital Presence
Aranyam has an established website — built by our team — that presents the brand, menu, gallery, and
locations effectively. However, the existing digital infrastructure does not support direct online ordering,
catering configuration, or payment collection. Enquiries are primarily handled through phone calls or
WhatsApp — a process that requires significant manual effort and introduces risk of errors, delays, and
lost orders.
The Opportunity
The gap between Aranyam's brand reputation and its current digital ordering capability represents the
opportunity this platform is designed to address. A customer who has experienced Aranyam's food and
wants to book catering for their daughter's engagement should be able to do so entirely on their phone
— in under five minutes, with full confidence in pricing, menu options, and availability. That experience
does not exist today. This platform builds it.

08 / SMART RECOMMENDATION ENGINE
Smart Recommendation Engine
The recommendation engine is the intelligence layer of the platform — the feature that transforms a
menu-browsing experience into a personalised catering advisor. Rather than presenting every dish to
every customer and expecting them to configure their own order from scratch, the engine analyses the
customer's inputs and surfaces the options most likely to match their needs.

What the Engine Recommends
• Curated platters best suited to the occasion, headcount, and budget tier.
• Service type — buffet, live counter, or counter service — based on guest count and occasion
formality.
• Menu item combinations with balanced veg/non-veg ratios aligned to the dietary split.
• Add-on options and upgrades relevant to the occasion type.
A Practical Example
• Customer inputs: 200 guests, wedding, dinner, Hyderabad delivery, ₹600 per person, 60%
veg / 40% non-veg.
• The engine surfaces: 3 curated platters at the ₹500–700 range, recommends a buffet service
format, presents a balanced menu of 8 veg and 5 non-veg items, and offers a live counter
upgrade as an add-on.
• The customer configures their entire wedding catering order in under 10 minutes — without
a single phone call.

11 / SECURITY & SCALABILITY
Security & Scalability
Security
• All API endpoints protected by authentication tokens (JWT-based). Unauthenticated requests are
rejected at the API gateway.
• Customer data is stored in a managed, encrypted PostgreSQL database. Sensitive fields (payment
tokens, personal details) are never stored in plaintext.
• Payment processing is handled entirely by Razorpay — no card or UPI credentials pass through or
are stored on the platform's servers.
• OTP authentication reduces account compromise risk compared to password-based systems.
• Role-based access control in the admin dashboard ensures staff members access only the
functions relevant to their role.
• HTTPS enforced across all client-server communications.
Scalability
• The backend API is stateless — horizontally scalable as order volume grows.
• Supabase (PostgreSQL) provides managed database scaling, connection pooling, and automated
backups.
• The mobile application is designed with offline-resilient patterns — gracefully handling
intermittent connectivity common in Indian networks.
• The architecture supports the addition of new locations, new menu categories, and new business
units without structural rework.
• Firebase Cloud Messaging handles notification delivery at scale — from tens to hundreds of
thousands of users without infrastructure changes.
Data & Privacy
• Customer data is collected, stored, and used in accordance with applicable Indian data protection
standards.
• Customers have the ability to manage their profile data and request deletion from within the
application.
• Payment data handling adheres to Razorpay's PCI-DSS compliant infrastructure.

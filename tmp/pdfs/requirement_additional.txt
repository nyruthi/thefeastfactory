Catering & Event Food Ordering Platform
MVP Feature Scope Document
Project Overview
The proposed solution is a mobile and web-based catering management platform that enables
customers to plan events, customize menus, place catering orders, make payments, and track
deliveries. The platform also provides an administrative dashboard for managing vendors,
menus, customers, and orders.
The MVP focuses on delivering the core functionality required to launch, validate the business
model, and start acquiring customers.
